A checkpoint for your AI assistant.

More businesses let AI assistants answer customers, update orders and handle records. AgentShield checks every action the AI wants to take, stops the ones it shouldn’t, and keeps a record of what happened.

Think of the AI as a new employee

A new hire follows instructions fast, but can be tricked by a convincing note or a wrong request. You wouldn’t give them every key on day one. AgentShield is the manager who approves each move before it happens.

  • 1. The AI asksIt says what it wants to do, such as “look up this customer request.”
  • 2. AgentShield checksIs this person allowed to do that? Does the record belong to them?
  • 3. Approved or stoppedAllowed actions go through. The rest are blocked, and every decision is logged.

Where a small business would use it

If an AI tool can touch customer information, orders or money, it needs a checkpoint.

  • Online storeA chat assistant that can issue refunds or change orders.
  • Clinic or salonA booking assistant that reads and edits client appointments.
  • Accounting and bookkeepingAn assistant that reads invoices and records, where a hidden instruction in a document could cause harm.
  • Agencies and consultantsAn AI that handles several clients’ files and must never mix them up.
  • Software companiesTeams adding an AI assistant to their product. Their business customers increasingly ask for proof it has been tested.

Plain-English glossary

AI agent
An AI assistant that doesn’t just chat. It can take actions, like editing a record or issuing a refund.
Hidden instruction attack (prompt injection)
Sneaky text inside a message or document that tells the AI to do something its owner never asked for.
Separate customer accounts (multi-tenant)
When one system serves many businesses, each one’s data must stay private from the others.
Audit record
A log of what the AI asked for, what was decided and why, like a receipt for every action.
AgentShield is an early prototype. Our results come from small practice tests and do not guarantee that any AI is safe. It is not a security certification.

🏎️ Boulevard Racer

An endless city-boulevard drive with real tire-slip physics. Dodge traffic, grab coins, ride the green lights. Steer with ←/→ or A/D, gas with ↑/W, brake with ↓/S. On mobile, use the on-screen pedals.
